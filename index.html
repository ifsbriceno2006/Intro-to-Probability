1 · The Language of Probability

Experiment = a planned chance activity (flip a coin). Outcome = one possible result (H). Sample space S = the set of all outcomes. For one coin flip, S = {H, T}: the two outcomes are heads (H) and tails (T). Event = any collection of outcomes (e.g. "at most one head"). P(A) = probability of event A.

Probabilities are always between 0 and 1: P = 0 never happens, P = 1 always happens, P = 0.5 is a fair toss-up.

Equally likely outcomes: P(A) = (outcomes in A) ÷ (outcomes in S)

Example: toss a dime and a nickel. S = {HH, HT, TH, TT}. A = "exactly one head" = {HT, TH}, so P(A) = 2/4 = 0.5.

🔬 Law of Large Numbers. Flip a fair coin and watch the relative frequency of heads settle toward 0.5.

2 · OR, AND & Complement (Venn Diagrams)

A OR B (union, A ∪ B): in A, in B, or in both. A AND B (intersection, A ∩ B): in both at once. Complement A′: everything in S that is not in A, so P(A) + P(A′) = 1.

3 · Mutually Exclusive vs Independent

Mutually exclusive: A and B cannot happen together, so P(A AND B) = 0 (their circles don't overlap).
Independent: knowing one happened doesn't change the other: P(A|B) = P(A), or equivalently P(A AND B) = P(A)·P(B).

⚠️ These are not the same. In fact, if A and B are mutually exclusive and both have positive probability, they are dependent: if A occurs, B is impossible!

Replacement: sampling with replacement gives independent picks; without replacement gives dependent picks.

4 · Conditional Probability

P(A|B) means "probability of A given B has already happened." The condition shrinks the sample space to B.
P(A|B) = P(A AND B) ÷ P(B)where P(B) > 0

Die roll: A = {2,3}, B = even = {2,4,6}. Within B only 2 is in A, so P(A|B) = 1/3.

5 · The Two Basic Rules

Addition Rule (OR)
P(A OR B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A AND B)
If mutually exclusive: just P(A) + P(B).
Multiplication Rule (AND)
P(A AND B) = P(B)·P(A|B)
If independent: P(A)·P(B).

Worked example (Felicity): P(Math) = 0.2, P(Speech) = 0.65, P(M|S) = 0.25.
P(M AND S) = 0.25 × 0.65 = 0.1625. P(M OR S) = 0.2 + 0.65 − 0.1625 = 0.6875. Since P(M|S) = 0.25 ≠ 0.2 = P(M), they are not independent; and P(M AND S) ≠ 0, so not mutually exclusive.
